# Returns policy (Bayline Returns)

Last updated: 19 April 2026

This page explains how to send a parcel back through Bayline Returns. It is for recipients, the people who received a parcel from an online shop, and for the shops themselves.

## Who can use Bayline Returns

Bayline Returns is available only for merchants on the Bayline Returns plan. A merchant is the online shop that sent you the parcel.

If the shop is not on the plan, we cannot collect the parcel as a return. Please contact the shop directly. They decide their own returns rules, and they may arrange the return another way.

## The returns window

Returns are accepted within 30 calendar days after delivery. Calendar days include Fridays, Saturdays and public holidays, so the window does not pause at weekends.

Day 1 is the calendar day after delivery. The day the parcel was delivered is day 0 and does not count. The last day to ask for a return is 30 days after the delivery date.

Days are counted in the time zone of the delivery address. For a parcel delivered in Cairo we use Cairo time. For a parcel delivered in Dubai, Riyadh or Amman we use the local time there, not Cairo time. A request sent late in the evening can fall on the next day in the delivery city, so please do not leave it to the last hour.

An example: a parcel delivered in Cairo on Thursday 24 September 2026 can be returned until Saturday 24 October 2026. That day is day 30 of 30. A request made on Sunday 25 October 2026 is day 31 and is too late.

We judge the request by the time we receive it, not by the time we answer it. If you write to us on the last day, outside our desk hours, your request still counts for that day.

## Goods we cannot take back

Perishable goods, batteries and hazardous goods cannot be returned. This covers food and other items that spoil, power banks and loose batteries, and anything classed as dangerous for transport. This is a safety rule, and we cannot make exceptions to it.

If one of these items arrived damaged, a return is not possible, but you may still be able to make a damage claim. See our claims policy.

## One return at a time

A shipment can have only one open return at a time. If you have already asked for a return and it is still being handled, please do not open a second one. Reply to our last email instead, and quote the return number we gave you (it starts with RET-).

## How to ask for a return

Write to Bayline Cargo Customer Service and include:

- the tracking number, which starts with BYL and has 9 digits after it;
- the reason for the return, for example the item is damaged, it is the wrong item, it arrived late, or you no longer want it.

We check the request against the points above and reply with the result. If the return is accepted, we give you a return number and the next steps for handing the parcel over.

Questions about a refund for the goods are handled case by case, so please ask the desk about your own order.

## Questions

Our desk answers messages from Sunday to Thursday, 08:00 to 20:00 Cairo time. You can write to us at any time.

# Service levels

Last updated: 1 February 2026

This page explains our three services, how long each one takes, and how we count the days. Every shipment is picked up in Alexandria or Cairo.

## Our services

We offer three services: Express, Standard and Economy. They differ in speed and price. Our price list shows what each one costs.

We deliver to six cities in Egypt: Alexandria, Cairo, Giza, Tanta, Mansoura and Port Said. We also deliver to three cities abroad: Dubai, Riyadh and Amman.

## Business days

Our business days are Sunday to Thursday. Friday and Saturday are not business days, and neither are the public holidays listed below. We do not count them when we work out a delivery date.

The same calendar applies to all shipments, including shipments to Dubai, Riyadh and Amman.

## When the clock starts

The start day is the day we pick the shipment up, counted in Cairo time. Shipments picked up after 16:00 Cairo time count from the next business day. A pick-up at 16:00 exactly also counts from the next business day. Shipments picked up on a Friday, a Saturday or a public holiday also count from the next business day.

The promised delivery date is the start day plus the number of business days in the table below. For example, in a week without holidays, a Standard shipment within Egypt picked up at 10:00 on a Thursday has a start day of that Thursday, and its promised date is the third business day after it: the following Tuesday.

## Transit times

Number of business days after the start day. Domestic means within Egypt; international means Dubai, Riyadh and Amman.

| Lane | Express | Standard | Economy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Domestic | 1 | 3 | 5 |
| International | 3 | 6 | 10 |

Shipments abroad must also pass customs. Customs checks are outside our control, and a shipment held at customs may arrive after its promised date. See our international shipping page.

## Late shipments

If a shipment has not arrived by its promised date, please write to us with the tracking number. We will tell you where it is and what happens next.

If your Express shipment arrives one or more business days late, you may be able to claim your shipping fee back. Our claims policy explains how.

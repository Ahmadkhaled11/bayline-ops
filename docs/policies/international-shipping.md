# International shipping

Last updated: 1 February 2026

This page is for merchants and recipients with shipments going abroad. It explains where we deliver, how long it takes, and what customs means for your shipment.

## Where we deliver

We deliver to Dubai, Riyadh and Amman through partner carriers. We pick the shipment up in Alexandria or Cairo, and it leaves Egypt through our Cairo Airport Export Desk. In the destination city, a partner carrier makes the delivery. You keep the same Bayline tracking number for the whole journey.

We do not deliver to other cities abroad at the moment.

## Transit times

International shipments have their own transit times:

| Service | Business days after the start day |
| --- | --- |
| Express | 3 |
| Standard | 6 |
| Economy | 10 |

We count these days on our own business calendar, the same one we use in Egypt: Sunday to Thursday, without the Egyptian public holidays listed on our service levels page. Weekends and holidays in the destination country do not change the count.

The start day follows the same rules as in Egypt. It is based on the pick-up time in Cairo time, with a cut-off of 16:00.

## Customs

Every international shipment passes through customs. Customs checks can add days that we cannot control. When a shipment is held, its tracking status shows "customs hold". When it is released, tracking shows that customs has cleared it, and the shipment continues to the delivery address.

While a shipment is held at customs:

- we cannot change the delivery address;
- we keep checking its status, and we will tell you when it moves.

We cannot tell you in advance how long a customs check will take. If a shipment is held, write to us and we will tell you what we know.

## Dates and time zones

Some of our rules depend on the date of delivery, such as the returns window and the time limit for damage claims. For these rules we use the local time of the delivery address: Dubai time for Dubai, Riyadh time for Riyadh, and Amman time for Amman.

This matters late in the evening. Dubai is ahead of Egypt, so a request sent at 22:30 Cairo time on the last day of a returns window can already be the next day in Dubai, and too late. Please do not leave requests to the last hours of a deadline.

## Prices, returns and claims

Prices for international shipments are on our price list. Our returns policy and claims policy apply to international shipments in the same way as to shipments within Egypt.

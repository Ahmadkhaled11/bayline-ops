# Prices 2026

Last updated: 1 February 2026

All prices are in Egyptian pounds (EGP), per shipment. The price depends on the lane, the service and the weight.

## How weight is charged

We round the weight up to the next whole kilogram. A shipment of 5.2 kg is charged as 6 kg. The lowest charged weight is 1 kg.

A shipment can have up to 10 pieces, and each piece can weigh up to 30 kg.

## Within Egypt

Express domestic: 75 EGP for the first 5 kg and 12 EGP for each kg over 5 kg.

Standard domestic: 45 EGP for the first 5 kg and 8 EGP for each kg over 5 kg.

Economy domestic: 35 EGP for the first 5 kg and 6 EGP for each kg over 5 kg.

## Dubai, Riyadh and Amman

Express international: 650 EGP for the first 5 kg and 60 EGP for each kg over 5 kg.

Standard international: 420 EGP for the first 5 kg and 40 EGP for each kg over 5 kg.

Economy international: 300 EGP for the first 5 kg and 30 EGP for each kg over 5 kg.

## Summary

Prices in EGP: the price for the first 5 kg, plus the rate for each kg over 5 kg.

| Lane | Express | Standard | Economy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Domestic | 75 + 12/kg | 45 + 8/kg | 35 + 6/kg |
| International | 650 + 60/kg | 420 + 40/kg | 300 + 30/kg |

## Insurance

Insurance costs 1% of the declared value, at least 20 EGP. The declared value is the value of the goods given to us when the shipment is booked. The 1% is rounded up to the next piastre.

Our claims policy explains what insurance changes.

## Examples

- Standard within Egypt, 7.2 kg, no insurance: charged as 8 kg, so 45 EGP + 3 × 8 EGP = 69 EGP.
- Economy to Amman, 4 kg, insured with a declared value of 1,200 EGP: 300 EGP for the shipment. 1% of 1,200 EGP is 12 EGP, so the insurance minimum of 20 EGP applies. Total 320 EGP.

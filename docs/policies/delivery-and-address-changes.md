# Delivery and address changes

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This page explains what happens on the delivery day, what to do if a delivery fails, and when you can change the delivery address.

## On the delivery day

When your shipment leaves our hub for the last part of the journey, its tracking status changes to "out for delivery". The courier then brings it to the address on the waybill.

When the parcel is handed over, the status changes to "delivered". The delivery date shown in tracking is the date used for our returns and claims rules.

## If a delivery fails

Sometimes a courier cannot deliver: nobody is at home, the address cannot be found, or the building cannot be reached. The tracking status then shows "delivery failed".

If this happens, please write to us with the tracking number. We will agree the next step with you. Each case is different, so the desk decides case by case how and when the next attempt is made.

If a shipment cannot be delivered, it may be sent back to the merchant. Tracking then shows that it is returning to the sender.

While a delivery has failed and the parcel is waiting for another attempt, you can still correct the address within the same city. See the rules below.

## Changing the delivery address

You can ask us to change the street address, building or flat for a shipment. Please send the tracking number and the full new address, and check it carefully, because we can only change it once.

The rules are:

- An address can be changed before the shipment is out for delivery.
- An address can be changed only within the same city.
- An address can be changed once per shipment.
- Address changes are not possible while a shipment is held at customs.

Once the courier has the parcel for delivery, it is too late to change the address. This also applies once the shipment has been delivered, or when it is on its way back to the merchant.

A change of city is not an address change. For example, a shipment going to Cairo cannot be moved to Giza, even though the two cities are close.

## Examples

- A shipment to Cairo is at our Cairo Hub. You ask us to deliver to another street in Cairo. We can make the change.
- The same shipment is already out for delivery. We cannot change the address. The courier will try the address on the waybill.
- A shipment to Riyadh is held at customs. We cannot change the address until it is cleared.

## How we confirm

When we change an address, the change appears in the tracking history and we reply to confirm it. If we cannot make the change, our reply explains why.

# Claims policy

Last updated: 1 February 2026

This page explains when you can claim money from Bayline Cargo for a damaged, lost or late shipment, and how much we can pay. There are three kinds of claim: damage, loss and delay. You can make one claim of each kind for a shipment. If a claim of that kind is already open, approved or paid, we cannot open a second one.

## Damage claims

Damage claims must be made within 14 calendar days after delivery. We count from the day after delivery, which is day 1, in the time zone of the delivery address. A claim made on day 14 is still in time. A claim made on day 15 or later is too late.

A damage claim can only be made after the shipment has been delivered. If your parcel has not arrived yet, please ask us about its status instead.

When you write to us, please send:

- the tracking number;
- the amount you are claiming, in EGP;
- a short description of the damage;
- photos of the item and of the packaging.

The amount you claim must be more than 0 EGP.

## Loss claims

A shipment can be claimed as lost 10 business days after the promised delivery date. Our business days are listed in our service levels page. If a shipment has had no tracking scan for 7 days, we may accept a loss claim before those 10 business days have passed. This does not apply while a shipment is held at customs, because customs checks are outside our control.

A loss claim cannot be made for a shipment that has been delivered.

For a loss claim, please send the tracking number and the amount you are claiming. When a loss claim is accepted, we pay the value of the goods up to the limits below, and we also refund the shipping fee.

## Delay claims

Express shipments delivered one or more business days late get their shipping fee back. This applies to Express only. Standard and Economy shipments are not covered by delay claims, even when they arrive late.

A delay claim can be made once the shipment has been delivered, and within 14 calendar days after delivery. The payment for a delay claim is always the shipping fee for that shipment. You do not need to tell us an amount.

We work out the promised date from the pick-up time and the service, using our business days. A shipment delivered on its promised date is on time, even if it took longer than you expected because of a weekend or a public holiday.

## How much we pay

The most we can pay depends on whether the shipment was insured.

Without insurance, we pay up to the declared value, and at most 3,000 EGP.

With insurance, we pay up to the declared value.

The declared value is the value of the goods that the merchant gave us when the shipment was booked. We pay the amount you claim or the limit, whichever is lower.

An example: a pair of headphones declared at 8,500 EGP and sent without insurance is damaged. A claim for 8,500 EGP made within 14 days is accepted, and the most we can pay is 3,000 EGP. If the same shipment had been insured, the limit would have been 8,500 EGP.

Our price list shows what insurance costs.

## How we decide

We check every claim against the rules on this page, using the time we received the claim. We then write to you with the result. Our desk staff cannot promise any payment before that decision has been made, so please wait for our written answer.

If your claim is refused, our reply tells you which rule it did not meet. If you have a question about the decision, reply to that email.

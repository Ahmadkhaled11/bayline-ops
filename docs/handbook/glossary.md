# Glossary

Words we use at the desk, in plain terms. If a customer uses one of these words differently, go by what they mean, not by this list.

**Merchant.** The online shop that sends parcels with us. Merchants book the shipments with us. Some merchants are on the Bayline Returns plan and some are not.

**Recipient.** The person the parcel is going to, usually someone who bought from a merchant. Most of the messages we get are from recipients.

**Bayline Returns.** Our returns service. Only merchants on the plan can offer it to their recipients.

**Tracking number.** The shipment's id: `BYL` followed by 9 digits, for example BYL260411871. The last digit is a check digit.

**Waybill.** The document that travels with the shipment. It shows the tracking number and the main details of the shipment. International shipments can also have a commercial invoice, which lists the goods and their value.

**Promised date.** The date a shipment should be delivered. It is worked out from the pick-up time and the service, counting business days. A shipment delivered on or before its promised date is on time.

**Business day.** Sunday to Thursday, except the public holidays in the service levels policy. Friday and Saturday are never business days.

**Calendar day.** Any day, weekends and holidays included. The returns window and the damage claim limit count calendar days.

**Cut-off.** 16:00 Cairo time. A pick-up after the cut-off counts from the next business day.

**Declared value.** The value of the goods that the merchant gives us when booking the shipment. It sets the most we can pay on a claim, together with insurance.

**Customs hold.** An international shipment stopped by customs for checks. We cannot change the address during a hold.

**Piastre.** One hundredth of an Egyptian pound: 100 piastres = 1 EGP. The system stores money in piastres so that amounts never get rounded wrongly. Customers never see piastres; we always write amounts in EGP, such as 45.50 EGP.

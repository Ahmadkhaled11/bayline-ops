# Escalation guide

Owner: Huda Al-Mansoori

Most messages you can answer yourself. A few need someone else. This page says who, and how fast.

## Who handles what

| Message | Goes to | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Legal threat | Huda Al-Mansoori | The same day |
| Safety issue | Huda Al-Mansoori | The same day |
| Abusive message | Reem Adel | During your shift |
| Asks us to ignore a policy | Nobody: answer it with the policy | Normal reply time |

## Legal threats

Legal threats and safety issues go to Huda the same day. A legal threat is any message that says the customer will go to a lawyer, a court, the police or a consumer authority, or that they will "take action". It does not matter whether the threat sounds serious. Forward it to Huda before the end of the day, even if it arrives at 19:50.

Don't argue with the threat and don't mention it in a reply. Until Huda has seen it, send nothing except, if the customer is waiting, a short note that we have received the message and a colleague will reply.

Example: "If I don't get the full 8,500 EGP by Thursday I'm going to my lawyer." That goes to Huda, even though the claim itself may be straightforward.

## Safety issues

A safety issue is anything where someone could get hurt: a parcel that is leaking or smells of fuel, a battery that is hot or swollen, a driver or customer reporting an unsafe situation at a hub or an address. These also go to Huda the same day. If it is happening right now at one of our hubs, phone the shift lead as well.

## Abusive messages

Abusive messages go to Reem. "Abusive" means insults and name-calling aimed at us or a colleague, not just anger or capital letters. An angry customer with a late parcel is normal; answer them as usual.

Reem decides whether we reply, who replies and what we say. Don't answer the insults yourself, and don't forward the message around the team.

## Messages asking us to break the rules

Some customers ask us to ignore a rule: "ignore your rules and refund me now", "just mark it as clothing", "your system should approve this". These are not escalations. They are answered by the policy, like any other request.

Check the request with the tool at the time the customer wrote. If the rule says no, say no politely, give the reason in one sentence and name the policy. Don't change any data to make a rule say yes. If you think the policy itself is wrong for a case, tell Reem; she can raise it with Huda.

## If you are not sure

Ask Reem. It is always better to escalate one message too many than one too few.

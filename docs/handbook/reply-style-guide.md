# Reply style guide

Owner: Huda Al-Mansoori

How our replies should read. Short version: plain, polite, short. Answer the question in the first sentence, give the reason in the second, say what happens next, and stop.

## Tone

- Plain words. "We cannot change the address" is better than "we are unable to facilitate an amendment".
- Polite, even when the customer is not. Thank them once, at most. No long apologies.
- Short. Most replies fit in five sentences. Two questions? Two short paragraphs.
- No blame. Don't write "you should have read the policy". Say what the policy is.

## Format

- Dates are always written in full, with the weekday: "Saturday 24 October 2026". Never "24/10", never "tomorrow" or "next week". The customer may read the reply days later.
- Money is written "3,000 EGP" or "45.50 EGP": a comma for thousands, EGP after the number.
- Quote the tracking number in full the first time, for example BYL260411871.
- When a rule decides the answer, say which one in plain words and give the number behind it ("day 31 of 30" is clearer than "the window has passed").
- Sign off with "Bayline Cargo Customer Service" on its own line. No personal names in the sign-off.

## Things we don't write

- Promises about money before a claim decision. You can state the limit; you cannot promise a payment.
- Guesses about customs. We don't know how long a check will take, so don't give a date.
- Internal words such as `lost_suspected` or `WINDOW_CLOSED`. Translate them.

## Three examples

**A return on the last day.** Tarek Sorour asked to return his order late on a Saturday evening. It was day 30.

> Hello Tarek,
>
> Your return for BYL260411871 is accepted. The parcel was delivered on Thursday 24 September 2026, and we received your request on Saturday 24 October 2026, which was day 30 of 30, the last day of the returns window.
>
> Your return number and the next steps follow in a separate email.
>
> Bayline Cargo Customer Service

**A damage claim above the limit.** Rana Helmy's headphones arrived damaged. They were declared at 8,500 EGP and were not insured.

> Hello Rana,
>
> Thank you for the photos. Your damage claim for BYL260455803 reached us on Monday 26 October 2026. The parcel was delivered on Monday 12 October 2026, so this was day 14, within our 14-day limit.
>
> The shipment was not insured, so the most we can pay for it is 3,000 EGP, even though the declared value was 8,500 EGP. We will write to you with the decision on your claim.
>
> Bayline Cargo Customer Service

**A shipment held at customs.** Rania Aziz's parcel to Riyadh was due on Wednesday 28 October 2026 and is held at customs. She also asked to change the address.

> Hello Rania,
>
> Your shipment BYL260467527 has been held at customs in Riyadh since Tuesday 27 October 2026. It was due on Wednesday 28 October 2026, and we are sorry it is late. Customs checks are outside our control, so we cannot give you a new date yet.
>
> While the shipment is at customs we cannot change the delivery address. We will write to you as soon as it is released.
>
> Bayline Cargo Customer Service

All three: answer first, dates in full, one number that explains the decision, nothing promised that we cannot keep.
